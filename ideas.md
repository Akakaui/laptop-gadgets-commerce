# Kora Commerce visual directions

## Direction A — Quiet hardware editorial (selected)

An off-white showroom canvas with graphite navigation, lime operational accents, configuration-first product cards, and precise specs. It communicates trust and competence without pretending to be a luxury marketplace. This is the chosen direction because it can be rebranded for laptop, phone, and accessory dealers while remaining legible on low-cost mobile devices.

## Direction B — Lagos night market

A dark, high-contrast storefront with electric orange and blue, oversized product photography, and quick-buy patterns. It could work for gaming and youth-focused inventory but risks feeling promotional, noisy, and less credible for business laptops and refurbished devices.

## Direction C — Enterprise service desk

A light, dense interface built around comparison tables, warranty policies, business procurement, and quote requests. It would support B2B buyers well but would make everyday consumer browsing feel too formal and administrative.

## Selection principle

Kora uses Direction A for the storefront and an operational variant of the same system for the admin. The visual language stays coherent across the customer and dealer surfaces while the density changes with the task.
